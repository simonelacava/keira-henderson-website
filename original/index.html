How I work

Every project is built around the decision you need to make and follows the MRS Code of Conduct.

  1. Step 1

    Frame the question

    We agree the decision the research needs to inform, and what a useful answer looks like.

  2. Step 2

    Choose the right method

    Qualitative, quantitative or both, designed to be inclusive and to fit your timeline and budget.

  3. Step 3

    Listen and analyse

    I run the fieldwork, then synthesise what people said and did into a clear story.

  4. Step 4

    Put it to work

    Workshops, presentations and tools that help your team use the findings day to day.

About me

Hi, I’m Keira.

I studied social anthropology at the University of Kent, which is where my interest in why people do what they do began. I started out doing face-to-face fieldwork at Kantar Public. I then spent six years in customer insight at QVC, working across the UK, Germany and Italy and representing the customer with leadership teams.

Today I work independently with brands, public bodies and community organisations who want to understand their customers properly and act on what they learn.
